## Why these changes

**Why did we do this?**

M4 makes the battlefield feel alive: drivable vehicles, an animated soldier,
and a first-person view that actually holds a rifle and aims through an optic —
pushing from a playable tactical framework toward something that looks like a
game.

**What problem did it solve?**

Replacing the placeholder mannequin and boxy trucks, so that the player-held
rifle, muzzle flash, tracers and grenades read coherently in one frame; and
fixing the class of input bugs that only real hands expose, plus the silent
message-drop hazard of "events with no listener".

**What will it affect in the final deliverable?**

Established M4's visual and interaction baseline: real vehicle and soldier
models, the first-person weapon system and grenade gameplay all usable — the
foundation for swapping in the real rifle model and polishing the shooting
feel next.
